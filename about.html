About Us

Who We Are

Sports Corner website is the extension of the sports corner radio session from your favourite radio station. The website was established in 2025 as the way of engaging with our listeners not only on radio but also through web platform. Since when it was established the website managed to give uptodate sports news to the fans across the country.

Our Objective

Our objective is ensure the sports fans across the country get credible sports news anywhere on their demand including recorded sessions of their favourite sports corner radio program on the same platform. We aim to cut through the noise and deliver high-impact sports journalism. Our goal is to keep fans informed, engaged, and ahead of the game.

What We Offer

Welcome to your sports adventure with us whereby we will bring you credible information concerning different sports in the country and abroad. We follow different sports tournaments across the global sports landscape, offering dedicated updates. On football we provide match reports, transfer rumors and players news. On basketball we provide game highlights, player stats, and trade buzz. On boxing we we report analyse before and after different boxing matches. On tennis and golf we report and analyse on tour events and grand slams. Within the country and regional sports we reports on matches, transfer rumors and players news.

Meet the Team

Our team brings you closer to the games, teams, and athletes you care about most. Our content is driven by a passionate group of experience sports journalists who are sports fans as well. We combine strict editorial standards with a genuine love for sports culture to bring you stories that matter.
